# Escaro Labs

> **Per-issue bounties for open-source work, escrowed and settled on Stellar.**

Fund an issue, and the money sits in escrow until the work is proven. Escaro turns open-source bounties into a settlement problem the ledger can hold.

## At a glance

| Area | What it means |
| --- | --- |
| Escrow per issue | A bounty is funded up front and held by a Soroban contract until a maintainer confirms the merge. |
| Settlement on Stellar | Payouts move as USDC on Stellar, so contributors are paid without a middle layer holding the funds. |
| Proof in the open | Every escrow, release, and refund is a public transaction anyone can audit. |
